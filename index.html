Social Media Marketing Agency FAQs

What does a free audit from Eleven Media include?

A comprehensive teardown of your current profile bio, top 10 reels’ 3-second retention hooks, hashtag and SEO discovery index, and exactly 3 actionable changes you can implement immediately — whether you hire us or not.

What platforms do you manage?

We specialize in Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook. We focus strictly on the channels where short-form vertical attention and targeted regional discovery yield actual business inquiries — not just likes.

Can you increase my follower count?

Yes — but we focus on quality over quantity. We grow audiences that actually convert into leads, inquiries, and customers. We never use bots or engagement pods that inflate vanity numbers.

Do you work with businesses outside Kota?

Yes. While our agency is rooted in Kota, Rajasthan, we manage client accounts remotely across India via asynchronous video updates, shared creative staging folders, and weekly sprint debriefs on WhatsApp.

How does reporting work?

Bi-weekly visual dashboards highlighting core metrics: qualified inbound messages, hook retention rates, and top-performing formats. We never obscure bad weeks or hide behind meaningless vanity impressions.

What’s the process to get started?

Step 1: Submit your handle via our free audit form below or on WhatsApp. Step 2: Receive our video and profile breakdown. Step 3: A brief scope alignment conversation. Step 4: Kickoff and scheduling within 3 business days.
